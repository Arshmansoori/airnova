Health Advisory & Precautionary Measures (AQI.in Health Model)
Actionable guidance for Delhi NCR
😷
Protective Mask
Recommended Outdoors
Wear an N95 or FFP2 rated particulate respirator when commuting or walking along arterial traffic corridors. Cloth masks do not filter PM2.5.
🪟
Indoor Air & Purifier
Keep Windows Sealed
Seal windows during early morning and late night boundary layer inversions. Operate true HEPA air purifiers in sleeping quarters.
🏃
Outdoor Exercise
Avoid 6 AM – 9 AM
Thermal inversion traps severe smog at ground level early morning. Shift running or intense cardio indoors or after 12:00 PM when sun heats the air.
👶
Sensitive Cohorts
High Vigilance
Asthmatic individuals, young children, and elderly citizens should limit unneeded outdoor transit. Keep bronchodilator rescue inhalers at hand.
